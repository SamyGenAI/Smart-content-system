# How Jeff Su, Nate Herk and Ben AI script their videos

Research Samy ran on 2026-10-25 with vidIQ: full transcripts of 9 videos, 3 per creator, mixing breakout hits and recent uploads. Transcripts only, so editing, b-roll and thumbnails are not covered. 9 videos show patterns, not rules. This is why `youtube-writer` is built the way it is.

## The intro

| | Jeff Su | Nate Herk | Ben AI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Length | 20 to 30 s | 15 to 45 s | 30 to 35 s |
| First line | Who the video is for, or the question they already have | A big number or claim, then "I tested it" | News from an authority, or "most people are doing it wrong" |
| Proof | Light: "3 years making AI videos" | His builds on screen in the first 20 s | A founder bio in every video |
| Promise | One outcome, phrased "so that…" | A list of benefits, then what you'll understand by the end | 3 or 4 specific deliverables |
| Handoff | "Let's get started." | "Let's not waste any time and get straight into this one." | "Starting with the first and most important one" |

**Jeff Su: qualify the viewer, then remove the fear.**

- "You're in the right place if…" (Cowork, 1.5M views): three traits of the viewer, then "by the end, you know exactly what it can do and where it fits."
- "If you already use X, you might be wondering Y" (Personal Agents): opens on the viewer's own doubt.
- Cold open, then a gap (AI Agents, 5.5M views, his biggest): a 15 s supercut of people saying "agent, agentic…", then the problem (explanations are "either too technical or too basic"), who it's for, a "1-2-3 learning path", and reassurance that the terms are "simpler than you think".
- Pattern: audience, frustration, learning path, reduce intimidation, "Let's get started." He never brags. He presents himself as a normal user.

**Nate Herk: scale, proof, stakes.**

- GitHub repos: "100,000+ repos… most are a waste of time. I tried 100+, here are the 10." Three payoffs (faster, cheaper, replaced me), then a money angle.
- Jev: "Jev is literally everywhere… I tested it on 12 use cases." A Chrome extension and a trading bot he built, on screen in the first 30 s.
- GrokBot: "25 concepts split across five parts", structure previewed right away.
- Pattern: volume claim, visible proof he did the work, a money or practical stake, catchphrase transition.

**Ben AI: borrow authority, then a bio.**

- Prompting rules (21.8× breakout): "I looked at Anthropic's new guides… almost everyone is prompting Claude 5 models wrong." Authority, contrarian claim, urgency.
- Small business plugin: "Anthropic just released 44 skills…", then a 4-part agenda.
- Every video has a bio at 0:17 to 0:30 that doubles as a pitch for his offer.

## The body

- **Jeff: concept ladder with one running example.** Levels or numbered capabilities ("Kicking things off at level one…"). One example through the whole video. Callbacks ("keep that in mind moving forward"). "Pro tip" boxes for jargon. Self-deprecating jokes every 1 or 2 minutes. Closes with a recap visual, and in comparisons a "which should you choose" guide.
- **Nate: numbered list with progress markers.** Each item: what it is, live demo, plain analogy ("a webhook is a doorbell"), how to use or sell it. Tells the viewer where they are ("19 down, 6 more to go"). Pivots between sections. Honesty notes ("not sponsored", "don't install all of these today"). Often a bonus item.
- **Ben: rule, why, demo, customize, automate.** Each item cites its source, often with a clip. Demos on his own business. A customization tip and a scheduling tip. Every item points to a free resource in the description.

## CTAs

| | Lead magnet | Paid pitch | Outro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jeff | About 4:00 (25 to 40% in), one sentence | Short, late, often with a self-aware joke | "If you found this helpful, watch X… have a great one." |
| Nate | About 4:45 (30% in), 30 s pitch for a free SOP, then "back to the video" | Light | "Like, it helps a ton… see you next one." |
| Ben | About 2:40, plus repeated "free resources" mentions | About 5:30, 30 to 45 s, repeated at the end | "Check out the video here above." No sign-off |

Ben's paid pitch is the heaviest and earliest. Jeff's is the lightest, and Jeff has the biggest outliers.

## What Samy takes

1. First line about the viewer, not himself.
2. Proof on screen before explanation. His consulting work and Smart OS builds are the proof.
3. One outcome with "so that".
4. Preview the structure. It lowers the effort the viewer expects and signals an end.
5. Intro under 35 s, ending on a fixed handoff line that becomes his signature.
6. One running example through the whole video.
7. Lead magnet at 25 to 30%, after the first payoff, one or two sentences. Paid pitch at the end or one honest line.

Intro formula: **who it's for / their doubt** → **contrarian or scale claim** → **10 s proof on screen** → **structure preview + "so that" outcome** → **handoff line**.
